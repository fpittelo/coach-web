Privacy Policy

Coach Web is a personal, single-tenant coaching application operated under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nLPD / FADP). This page explains exactly what is processed where, and how you stay in control.

Controller and scope

The controller is the owner-operator of this single-user instance, acting under Swiss nLPD/FADP. The public pages (/, /disclaimer, /privacy) collect zero personal data, set zero cookies, and run zero trackers or analytics.

Data in the authenticated console

After sign-in the application processes the identity claims you grant through Google Sign-In / Identity-Aware Proxy (email address, name, and avatar) solely to enforce the single-owner allowlist. It also processes workout and biometric telemetry read from Intervals.icu (FTP, heart rate, HRV, sleep, CTL/ATL/TSB) together with the objectives, preferences, post-session RPE and debriefs, and chat threads you author.

Google Cloud Platform hosting and security

The application is deployed on Google Cloud Platform — Cloud Run for the services and Cloud Storage for encrypted database snapshots. Transcripts and debriefs are encrypted at the application layer with Fernet, and database snapshots are encrypted at rest. Raw wellness telemetry remains authoritative in Intervals.icu and is held transiently in memory during active sessions only. The single session cookie is HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax and stores no coaching data.

Sub-processors and cross-border processing

Processing relies on Google Cloud Platform (hosting, Google OIDC and IAP authentication), Intervals.icu (training and wellness data source), and OpenRouter together with the routed large-language-model providers used to generate coaching responses. These providers may process data outside Switzerland; only the minimum necessary content is sent for each request.

Retention, erasure and your rights

Chat messages are auto-purged on a 90-day retention schedule, and inactive threads on a 365-day schedule. You can trigger a self-service synchronous Clear memory purge and reset your settings at any time from the console. Under the nLPD you may request access, correction or erasure of your data through the project repository at github.com/fpittelo/coach-web.
